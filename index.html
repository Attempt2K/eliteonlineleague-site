[03]System specs

01Bounty on every head

Every kill raises your price. Cross $1,000 and a diamond spins next to your name, and the whole server starts hunting you. Top the board, or be the one who takes the top dog down.

02Four teams, one town

Four teams fight over the flags at once, each from its own walled compound with gates, corridors and turret pads. Take them, hold them, outlast the countdown.

03Soldiers who fight back

Squads with leaders, medics who come running when you're hit, defenders, raiders and flag runners. Bots that dodge your grenades and shove you out of doorways.

04Read a class at a glance

Team colour on the suit, class colour on the armour plates. You know what you're up against before the first shot. Nine classes, four command ranks.

SoldierHeavyMedicEngineerInfiltratorMajorExecutionerAssault TrooperCorroder

05The armory

120+ weapons, each with its own model, sound and round: rifles, shotguns, lasers, flak, launchers, mines, gas, a gravity well, even a chip that turns an enemy turret on its own team. Run dry and you call the dropship home to restock.

06Dig in

Engineers on both sides build turret crates and wall kits: eight kinds of turret, walls in three strengths. Bullets bounce off a turret; only explosives bring it down.

07Return to sender

Turn incoming fire back on the shooter, or drop a charge behind an enemy and blast him straight into your squad's guns.

08Any weather, any hour

Midday sun, streetlights and muzzle flash at night, rain, and a Michigan snowfall.

09More than one map

The town and its shops, the office blocks of Downtown, and the Trenches: dug-in earthworks and brick ruins.

10Something got out

The Breach: a third side of monsters in tiers, with fireballs, ray guns and a relic to defend. They hunt every team, so any fight can turn into all-out war.

[04]Incident log

Autumn, 1992. A space company's ship comes down outside a small Michigan town: sodium streetlights, rental stores, beige computers and BMX bikes.

Something got out of the wreck. It learns to look like the people it takes. Now the town is a battlefield, the company wants its cargo back, and there is a price on everyone.
