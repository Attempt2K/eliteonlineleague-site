An old-school top-down team shooter, rebuilt modern. Hold the bases, run the flags, hunt the biggest bounty on the board, and fight beside a hundred soldiers who think.

What it is

Bounty on every head

Kill and your bounty climbs. The higher it gets, the more you are worth to everyone else. Be the big dog on the leaderboard, or the one who brings him down.

Bases and flags

Walled compounds with gates, corridors and turret pads. Take the flags, hold them all, and survive the countdown.

A hundred soldiers who think

Squads with leaders, medics who come when you are hit, defenders, raiders and flag runners. Bots that dodge your grenades and push you out of doorways.

Classes you read at a glance

Your team's colour is on your suit and your class's colour is on your armour plates, so you know what you are facing before the first shot.

SoldierHeavyMedicEngineerInfiltratorMajor

Gear up at the store

Rifles, shotguns, autocannons and plasma cannons, each in several models with their own price, sound and stats. Ammo runs out and grenades are five to a trip, so you call the dropship back to restock.

Build the base

Engineers carry turret crates and wall kits. Put up machine-gun turrets and walls where the fight is, and hold them until someone shoots them down.

Send it back

Turn incoming fire back on the shooter, or throw a charge behind an enemy and shove him straight into your squad's guns.

The story so far

A space company's ship comes down outside a small Michigan town in the autumn of 1992: sodium streetlights, rental stores, white desktop computers and BMX bikes.

Something got out of the wreck. It learns to look like the people it takes. Now the town is a battlefield, the company wants its cargo back, and there is a price on everyone.
